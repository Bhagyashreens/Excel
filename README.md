# Sales Performance Dashboard (Excel)

An interactive Excel dashboard that turns 2,000 sales orders into KPIs and insights by region, channel, product category and salesperson.

## Contents
- `sales_dashboard.xlsx` contains:
  - **Sales Dashboard**: KPI cards, charts and slicers
  - **Supporting_Pivots**: the PivotTables behind each dashboard element
  - **Sales_Data**: raw order data (2,000 rows)

## Dataset
Order ID, Date, Region, Sales Channel, Customer Type, Product Category, Product, Salesperson, Quantity, Unit Price, Discount, Revenue.

## KPIs
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total revenue | 2,452,084 |
| Total orders | 2,000 |
| Units sold | 5,552 |
| Average order value | 1,226 |

## Key Insights
- **Product mix:** Computers drive 55% of revenue (1.35M). Monitors follow at 21%.
- **Top products:** The top 5 products bring in 58% of revenue, led by the Gaming Laptop (433K).
- **Channels:** Online leads at 32%, then Corporate Sales (29%), Retail Store (26%) and Marketplace (13%).
- **Regions:** North is strongest (23%) and West is weakest (17%).
- **Seasonality:** Revenue peaks in Nov (274K) and Jun (259K) and dips in May and Jul (about 150K).

## Recommendations
1. Protect and grow the Computers range, since it carries the revenue.
2. Invest in Online and investigate why West underperforms.
3. Plan inventory and promotions around the Jun and Nov peaks.
